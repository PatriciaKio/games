Civic Pulse

Steward of the Storm — a resilience management sim

You are the new Mayor of Oakhaven, a mid-sized city modeled on real Chicago disasters. Over a 4-year term (16 seasons) you will face floods, heat waves, an industrial collapse, and the blizzard that ends every Chicago mayor's story.

Each season runs the real disaster-management cycle: Assess & Budget → Shock → Triage → Aftermath.

  • 💰 Budget — buy your way out of problems, until you can't.
  • 🤝 Social Capital (Bonding · Bridging · Linking) — community trust that absorbs chaos and makes recovery cheaper.
  • 🏗️ Hardiness — physical infrastructure that blunts damage.
  • 🗳️ Approval — fall below the line and you're impeached.

The trap: invisible prevention is thankless but cheap; visible reaction wins votes but costs a fortune. The best game isn't a disaster-free one — it's one where Oakhaven catches itself.

Research build · per-turn telemetry is recorded and exportable. Events are real, sourced Chicago history (see README).
